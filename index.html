📖 Chrono Core Operations Manual

1. Board & Initial Setup

Played on a 5×5 grid. Player 1 (Blue) starts at Row 5, moving upward. Player 2 (Red/AI) starts at Row 1, moving downward.

  • Row 1 (Red): Rush, Shift, General, Shift, Rush
  • Row 5 (Blue): Rush, Shift, General, Shift, Rush
  • Central Core: Placed at (C3 / 2,2) at the start of the match.

2. Piece Movements

  • ♛ General (1x): Moves 1 square in any of the 8 orthogonal or diagonal directions.
  • ▲ Rush (2x): Moves straight forward or diagonally forward any number of unobstructed squares. Cannot move backward or sideways.
  • ♞ Shift (2x): Moves 2 squares forward and 1 sideways (Knight/Keima jump). Can leap over friendly and enemy pieces.

3. Core Mechanics & Victory Conditions

  • Condition A: Capture the opponent's General.
  • Condition B: Move your piece carrying the Central Core into the opponent's back row (Row 1 for P1, Row 5 for P2).
  • Core Carrying: Moving onto a square with the Core automatically picks it up. If that piece is captured, the Core drops onto that square and the capturing piece claims it.

4. Time Echo (Temporary Drop)

  • Captured enemy pieces enter your Echo Stock.
  • On your turn, you may spend your action dropping an Echo piece onto any unoccupied square (except the opponent's back row).
  • Crucial Time Echo Rule: Dropped pieces only last for 1 turn. At the start of your NEXT turn, this piece automatically vanishes into the time stream! If it carried the Core, the Core drops on that square.
